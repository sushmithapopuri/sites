How to Play Tangrams

Objective

Arrange all 7 pieces (tans) to match the dark silhouette in the center of the board. The pieces must fit exactly inside the shape without overlapping!

Controls

Left Click & Drag Move a piece around the board.
Double Click Rotate the piece 45° clockwise.
Scroll Wheel (hover) Rotate the piece (Scroll Up/Down).
Right Click Flip / mirror the piece (e.g. Parallelogram).
Keyboard shortcuts R: Rotate 45° Clockwise
E: Rotate 45° Counter-Clockwise
F: Flip / Mirror selected piece

Game Modes

Puzzle Mode: Select puzzles from the library, view stats, and track completion progress.

Sandbox Mode: Create your own silhouettes, name them, save them to your library, and export them as JSON coordinates to share!
